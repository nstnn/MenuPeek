# Calendar

Calendar is a sidebar destination beside Today and Later. It replaces only the right content column with a pane that names the visible period, offers a Day/Week/Month switch (Week first), and moves with Previous, Today and Next. Calendar opens from Today or Later and leaves the selected list as it was. Day and Week show the saved blocks on a 24-hour time grid. Blocks are created, edited and deleted in a block editor popover; on the grid they can also be drawn by dragging, moved by dragging their body and resized by dragging their lower edge. Month shows a Monday-first day grid where each day lists its first blocks and `+N more`; choosing a day opens it in Day view. A failed read shows Calendar is unavailable with the period's message and Retry, never an empty calendar.

## Sub-features

- `calendar-entry`: the rail footer lists Calendar, a thin rule, History, Categories and Settings, in that order. Calendar uses the calendar symbol; History uses a clock symbol. The active entry is accent-colored, and History is not highlighted while Calendar is open.
- `calendar-route`: Calendar opens from Today and from Later, keeps the rail mounted, and leaves the Today/Later selection unchanged. Today, Later, History, Categories and Settings each leave it. Command-N and reopening the window from the menu bar return to the list.
- `calendar-guards`: like History, Calendar is disabled while a task edit, a task deletion confirmation, a category rename or a category removal confirmation is open. An idle category manager may be left for Calendar.
- `calendar-header`: the heading reads the period: Week `November 2026 · Week 47`, Day `November 2026 · Wednesday, November 18, 2026`, Month `November 2026`. Previous, Today and Next move by the current mode's unit; the view switch keeps the selected or current day in view.
- `calendar-grid`: Week shows seven day columns (Monday first) and Day one, under a fixed header row of weekday and date; today's date sits on an accent disc and its column is tinted. The grid scrolls vertically through 00:00–24:00 with an hour gutter, hour lines and dashed half-hour lines. On first appearance it scrolls to two hours before the current time when today is visible, else to 07:00; later period changes and reloads keep the scroll position.
- `calendar-blocks`: each saved block sits in its day's column from its start to its end (one point per minute at 100%). Overlapping blocks share the column side by side (halves, thirds); blocks that only touch keep full width. A block shows its title, then `10:00–11:00`, `10:00` or nothing as width allows; a title wraps only when its longest word fits, otherwise it truncates on one line. Blocks under 28 pt (the 15-minute block) show the title only. Hover shows the full title and time range.
- `calendar-now`: a red line with a dot marks the current minute across today's column only, refreshed each minute, and only while today's day is visible.
- `calendar-editor`: **New block** (header, top right) opens the editor under the button, prefilled with today when today is visible (else the selected day, else the first visible day), starting at the next whole hour on today (23:00 at the latest) or 09:00 on another day, one hour long. A click on empty grid space opens it on that day's 15-minute slot, one hour long (ending at 24:00 at the latest), with a dashed outline of the new block. A click, Return, Space or AXPress on a block opens it for editing. Fields: title, day (‹ › or Left/Right), From and To pop-ups of 15-minute times, and the duration below them. Changing From keeps the duration; choosing a To at or before From moves From earlier by the duration, so the range is always valid. The dashed outline follows the fields (for an edited block, once a field changes). Save (or Return) writes and closes; Cancel (or Escape) closes without writing; an empty title shows `Enter a title for the block.` and stays open; a failed write shows the state's message (`Could not save block. Try again.`, `Could not save block changes. Try again.`, `Could not delete block. Try again.`) and keeps the input. Delete… shows an inline `Delete block?` with Cancel focused; Cancel keeps the block. Focus returns to New block (after New block or a delete), to the edited block, or to the block a slot click created. A click outside the popover only closes it; the next click opens a new editor. While the editor is open, Calendar is busy like a task edit: the rail entries and the Today/Later switch are disabled, Command-N and Settings do nothing.
- `calendar-drag`: in Day and Week, dragging at least 4 pt on empty grid space draws a new block in the column where the drag started, snapped to 15 minutes (`10:00–11:30` shown inside a dashed accent outline); dragging up or down over the same span gives the same block, a span under one slot grows to 15 minutes, and the pointer above or below the day stops at 00:00 or 24:00. On release the editor opens with those times and the title focused; Save creates the row, Cancel or Escape creates nothing. Dragging a block's lower edge (over its bottom 2–6 pt the pointer becomes the compact frame-edge resize cursor on macOS 15 and later, the older up-down cursor on macOS 14, and keeps it while dragging) moves only its end, with the block faded under a live outline; release saves at once. Dragging above the start leaves a 15-minute block; releasing at the saved end writes nothing. Dragging a block's body (the closed-hand cursor once it has moved 4 pt) moves it with its duration kept: the block follows the pointer's travel from where it was grabbed, so it does not jump; the start snaps to 15 minutes and stops so the block stays within 00:00–24:00; in Week the column under the pointer picks the day, clamped to the visible week. The original is faded under a full-column outline with the new times; other blocks keep their layout until release. Release saves the new day and times at once; releasing where the block already is writes nothing. Escape during any drag drops it with nothing saved. Holding the pointer near or past the grid's top or bottom edge scrolls it while dragging. A plain click (under 4 pt of movement) on empty space, on a block or on its lower edge behaves as before (a block opens in the editor). The keyboard alternative to dragging is the editor: Return on a block, Left/Right on the Day field, and the From/To pop-ups. No drag starts while the editor, a task edit or a confirmation is open. A failed resize or move save leaves the block at its saved size and place and shows the state's message (`Could not save block changes. Try again.`) above the grid (`id=scheduleDragMessage`) until the next drag. Month has no drags.
- `calendar-month`: Month shows Mon … Sun columns with blank, inert padding cells before the 1st and after the last day, and every in-month day as one button: its date number (today on an accent disc, its cell tinted), then its blocks in time order as `10:00 Title` lines, then `+N more` when they do not all fit. The number of lines follows the cell height, which shares the pane height between the month's weeks: when all blocks fit they are all shown, otherwise the first ones and `+N more` for the rest, and at least one block line plus `+N more` always fit (a six-week month at the 800×560 minimum). Hover shows the full date and every block with its time range. A click, Return, Space or AXPress opens that day in Day view; switching back to Month outlines that day. Arrow keys move focus by day (Left/Right) or week (Up/Down) and step into the previous or next month at the edges. Previous and Next step by month.
- `calendar-unavailable`: a failed read shows `Calendar is unavailable`, `Could not load this week. Try again.` (day/month in those modes) and Retry.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Click Calendar at the bottom of the task window's rail, from Today or from Later.
- Leave by choosing Today or Later in the rail switch, or History, Categories or Settings.

## Driving it with fixture.sh + mpax

Preconditions:

- A fresh run launched with `--seed calendar --now 2026-11-18T15:00:00Z --tz America/Toronto` (Wednesday 18 November 2026, 10:00 local; the grid opens at 08:00). `--seed basic` works for the header and route checks and shows an empty grid. Take `$F snapshot <run> before-calendar`. `db.txt` does not list Calendar blocks yet; read them with `sqlite3 -readonly build/verify-menupocket/runs/<run>/data/fixture.sqlite "select title,day_key,start_minute,end_minute from schedule_blocks order by day_key,start_minute,created_at"`.

- **Sidebar order.** `$F ax <run> frame id=calendarEntry`, `frame id=taskHistoryEntry`, `frame id=manageCategories` and `frame "label=Open Settings"` have increasing `y`. `value id=calendarEntry` is labeled `Open Calendar`. The rule between Calendar and History is drawn only (not in the AX tree); confirm it in the screenshot.
- **Open from Today.** `$F ax <run> press id=calendarEntry`, then `wait id=schedulePane`. `value id=scheduleHeading` names the current week (`… · Week NN`), `value id=scheduleViewMode` reads `Week`, and `wait id=scheduleTimeGrid` succeeds. `value id=daybookDate` and `value id=todayProgress` still answer, and `value id=todayTab` is still selected. Snapshot `calendar-week`.
- **Navigate.** `press id=schedulePrevious`: the heading's week number drops by one. `press id=scheduleNext` twice: one week ahead. `press id=scheduleToday`: back to the first heading. Each heading change is the proof; `value id=schedulePrevious` is labeled `Previous week`.
- **Switch view.** `id=scheduleViewMode` is a radio group whose value is the current view. Choose a segment with `press "role=AXRadioButton,label=Day"` (or `Month`, `Week`); these are the only radio buttons with those labels in the window. Day: the heading includes the full date (`November 2026 · Wednesday, November 18, 2026`) and `id=schedulePrevious` is labeled `Previous day`. Day shows one `id^=scheduleDayHeader-` and only that day's blocks. Month: the heading is the month and year only and `wait id=scheduleMonthGrid` succeeds (see **Month grid**). Return to Week.
- **Open from Later.** `press id=laterTab`, `press id=calendarEntry`, `wait id=schedulePane`. `value id=laterTab` stays selected. `press id=laterTab` returns to Later's list (`wait id=laterTaskList`).
- **Leave through every entry.** From Calendar, `press id=taskHistoryEntry` shows `id=taskBrowserHeading` `History`; `press id=calendarEntry` returns; `press id=manageCategories` opens Categories; `press id=calendarEntry` returns; `press "label=Open Settings"` opens Settings; `press id=calendarEntry` returns; `press id=todayTab` shows `id=todayTaskList`.
- **Guards.** Start a task edit from a row's `…` > Edit, then `value id=calendarEntry` reports `[disabled]` and a press leaves the list. Cancel the edit; repeat with Delete… (before confirming) and with a category rename in the manager. Calendar becomes enabled again after each is cancelled.
- **Week grid.** `find "id^=scheduleDayHeader-"` lists seven headings Monday to Sunday labeled `Monday 16 November` …, with `, today` only on today. `find "id^=scheduleBlock-"` lists one element per `schedule_blocks` row, labeled `<title>, <Weekday> <d> <Month>, HH:mm to HH:mm` (for example `Design review, Wednesday 18 November, 10:00 to 11:00`), in day then start order; compare against the `sqlite3` rows. `frame` on two blocks: the `y` difference is their start difference in minutes times the zoom (Pairing is 30 pt below Design review at 100%), overlapping blocks have increasing `x` and do not intersect, and Thursday's three overlaps are each a third of the column. Screenshot the window and check titles truncate inside their blocks without covering the time text.
- **Month grid.** Choose Month. `find "id^=scheduleMonthDay-"` lists one AXButton per day of the month, 1st to last, and nothing for padding cells. `value id=scheduleMonthDay-<YYYY-MM-DD>` is labeled `<Weekday> <d> <Month>[, today], N blocks` (`no blocks`, `1 block`) and its value lists every block of that day in time order (`10:00 Design review, 10:30 Pairing, 12:00 Lunch, 13:00 Focus`); compare each day against the `sqlite3` rows for that `day_key`. `frame` on the 1st and on the Monday of the same week: the 1st is in the column its weekday gives (November 2026: the 1st is a Sunday, in the seventh column, and the month has six week rows). Screenshot the window at 100%: today's cell lists `10:00 Design review` and `+N more` with N equal to the blocks left out, and the day with three overlaps lists its first block line(s) and `+N more`; every line and `+N more` sit inside their cell. `key cmd+shift+equal` four times (150%, a layout taller than 560 pt) and screenshot: cells hold more lines and N shrinks accordingly; `key cmd+0`. `press id=scheduleMonthDay-<today>`: `value id=scheduleViewMode` reads `Day`, the heading names that day, and only its blocks are listed. Choose Month again: the same month is shown. `press id=scheduleNext` and `press id=schedulePrevious` change the heading by one month (`December 2026`, back to `November 2026`) and `value id=scheduleNext` is labeled `Next month`.
- **Current time.** `value id=scheduleCurrentTime` reads `Current time, 10:00` (the fixed clock). `press id=scheduleNext`: `find id=scheduleCurrentTime` finds nothing and no blocks are listed. `press id=scheduleToday` brings both back. In Day view on another day the line is absent.
- **Zoom and appearance.** `key cmd+shift+equal` four times reaches 150% (the window grows to 1200×872, a layout of 800×581 pt): `frame id=scheduleTimeGrid` stays inside the window's frame and `frame id=scheduleNext` and `frame id=scheduleDayHeader-<Sunday>` are inside it. `key cmd+0` returns the grid to its 100% width. Switch Theme to Light in Settings and back to compare both appearances.
- **Editor: create.** `press id=scheduleNewBlock`, `wait id=scheduleEditor`. With the fixed clock `value id=scheduleEditorDay` reads `Wednesday, November 18, 2026`, `id=scheduleEditorStart` `11:00`, `id=scheduleEditorEnd` `12:00`, `id=scheduleEditorDuration` `Duration 1 h`; `id=scheduleEditorTitle` is focused. `press id=scheduleEditorSave` with no title: `wait id=scheduleEditorMessage` reads `Enter a title for the block.`. `type "Write docs"`, `key return`, `gone id=scheduleEditor`: `find "id^=scheduleBlock-,label~=Write docs"` lists `Write docs, Wednesday 18 November, 11:00 to 12:00`, the `sqlite3` query shows `Write docs|2026-11-18|660|720`, and `value id=scheduleNewBlock` is `[focused]`.
- **Editor: edit.** `press "id^=scheduleBlock-,label~=Write docs"`, `wait id=scheduleEditor` (title `Edit block`, with `id=scheduleEditorDelete`). Times are native pop-ups: `press id=scheduleEditorStart` then, in the next call, `press "role=AXMenuItem,label=13:00"`; `wait "id=scheduleEditorEnd,label=14:00"` (the end followed). Choose End `12:00`: `wait "id=scheduleEditorStart,label=11:00"`. Choose End `14:45`; `press id=scheduleEditorNextDay` and `wait "id=scheduleEditorDay,label~=Thursday"`; `focus id=scheduleEditorTitle`, `key cmd+a`, `type "Write the docs"`; `press id=scheduleEditorSave`. The same `id` row now reads `Write the docs|2026-11-19|660|885`. `key return` reopens that block (focus returned to it; SwiftUI does not report `[focused]` on blocks), `key escape` closes it.
- **Editor: delete.** Open the block, `press id=scheduleEditorDelete`, `wait id=scheduleEditorDeleteConfirmation`; `press id=scheduleEditorCancelDelete` keeps the row; `press id=scheduleEditorDelete` then `press id=scheduleEditorConfirmDelete` removes it from the grid and from `schedule_blocks`.
- **Editor: write failure.** The fixture keeps its SQLite connection open, so `fail-open` does not reach writes. Instead hold a lock from another process for a few seconds, `(echo "BEGIN EXCLUSIVE;"; sleep 10; echo "ROLLBACK;") | sqlite3 <run>/data/fixture.sqlite &`, then press Save: `id=scheduleEditorMessage` shows the write message and the fields keep their values; after the lock is released, Save writes and closes. This is a simulated failure; log it in `actions.log`.
- **Editor: slot click.** `mpax` cannot click (mouse events posted to the fixture's PID do not reach SwiftUI; an attempt also opened an editor later without showing it). Ask the user to click empty space in a day column and type a title and Return, then read the block and row; also have them check that a click outside the open popover only closes it.
- **Drag create, resize and move.** `mpax` cannot post pointer drags (see the slot click above), so these are manual. Ask the user, on the `calendar` seed, to: draw from 10:00 to 11:30 on an empty column, check the outline reads `10:00–11:30`, type a title and press Return; then read the row (`<title>|<day>|600|690`). Drag Design review's lower edge from 11:00 to 12:15 (row `…|600|735`), then above its start (row `…|600|615`). Move Design review (Wednesday 10:00–11:00) by its body to Thursday 14:30 (row `Design review|2026-11-19|870|930`), check the outline reads `14:30–15:30` during the drag and the original is faded; drag a block above 00:00 and below 24:00 and check it stops with its duration kept; click a block's body without moving and check the editor opens. Start a draw, a resize and a move, press Escape before releasing, and compare the `sqlite3` rows before and after (unchanged). Hold a draw near the bottom edge and check the grid scrolls. For a failed resize or move, hold the `BEGIN EXCLUSIVE` lock described above and resize or move: `id=scheduleDragMessage` appears and the block is drawn at its saved size and place. Keyboard alternative (drivable): open a block with `press`, step the Day with `id=scheduleEditorNextDay`, choose a From time and Save; the row's day and times change with the duration kept. Record each as observed by the user or Not tested; the planners behind every gesture path are covered by `ScheduleDragSessionTests`.
- **Unavailable.** `$F fail-open <run> on`, stop and launch, `press id=calendarEntry`: `id=scheduleUnavailableState` and `id=scheduleLoadMessage` show, with `id=retrySchedule`. `$F fail-open <run> off`, `press id=retrySchedule`, and `wait id=scheduleTimeGrid`. This is a simulated storage failure.
- **Read-only.** Browsing writes nothing: compare `db.txt` from `before-calendar` with a snapshot taken after navigating.

## Gotchas

- Editor identifiers: `scheduleNewBlock`, `scheduleEditor` (the popover content; label `New block` or `Edit block`), `scheduleEditorTitle`, `scheduleEditorDay` (adjustable), `scheduleEditorPreviousDay`, `scheduleEditorNextDay`, `scheduleEditorStart`, `scheduleEditorEnd` (AXPopUpButtons), `scheduleEditorDuration`, `scheduleEditorMessage`, `scheduleEditorSave`, `scheduleEditorCancel`, `scheduleEditorDelete`, `scheduleEditorDeleteConfirmation`, `scheduleEditorCancelDelete` (label `Keep block`) and `scheduleEditorConfirmDelete` (label `Delete block`). The popover closes when the fixture deactivates: open it and act in consecutive calls.
- The pane's identifiers are `schedulePane`, `scheduleHeading`, `scheduleViewMode`, `schedulePrevious`, `scheduleToday`, `scheduleNext`, `scheduleUnavailableState`, `scheduleLoadMessage` and `retrySchedule`; the rail entry is `calendarEntry`. The grid is `scheduleTimeGrid`, with `scheduleDayHeader-<YYYY-MM-DD>` (AXHeading), `scheduleDayColumn-<YYYY-MM-DD>` (a container; its AX frame is the union of its blocks, not the full column), `scheduleBlock-<block UUID>` and `scheduleCurrentTime`. Month is `scheduleMonthGrid` (a container labeled with the month and year) with `scheduleMonthDay-<YYYY-MM-DD>` per in-month day (an AXButton; label is the date, `today` and the block count; value is every block as `HH:mm Title`, joined by `, `). Padding cells and the weekday row have no AX element.
- Month cells list block lines only up to the line count, which comes from the cell height (`ScheduleMonthSize`: chrome 30 pt, 14 pt per line, at least 2 lines); the AX value always lists every block, so the visible `+N more` is checked in the screenshot. Month cells do not create or edit blocks; New block in the header still works. Keyboard focus on Month cells (Tab, arrows, Return) did not respond to `mpax focus`/`key` on 2026-11-18; ask the user to check it by hand.
- Drag previews are drawn only and hidden from Accessibility; `scheduleDragMessage` is the only drag element in the AX tree.
- Blocks are AXButtons whose AXPress opens the editor; reach them by label (`"id^=scheduleBlock-,label~=Design review"`) because UUIDs change every run. The hour gutter and lines are drawn only and hidden from Accessibility.
- `mpax` cannot scroll. The grid opens two hours before the fixed clock; pick `--now` so the blocks you need are on screen, or read them through the AX tree, which lists off-screen blocks too.
- The 800×560 minimum and the compact rail (below 740 pt) need a window resize, which this harness cannot drive; 150% zoom gives an 800-pt-wide layout with the full rail as the closest check.
- `scheduleHeading` is one AX element whose label joins the two visible lines with ` · `.
- The view and period are kept for the session: leaving Calendar and returning shows the same view and period. They are not saved, so a relaunch starts on this week.
- Calendar does not create or refresh History, so History's first-visit day is unchanged by visiting Calendar first.
